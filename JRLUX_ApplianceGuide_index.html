01 · Laundry
Samsung Eco-Bubble Washer-Dryer
三星洗烘一体机
Model: EWD8060B · 8.0kg Wash / 6.0kg Dry
Washing 洗涤
1
Press the Power button to switch on.按电源键开机。
2
Load laundry — max wash 8.0kg, max dry 6.0kg. Close the door firmly.放入衣物——最大洗涤8.0公斤，最大烘干6.0公斤。关紧机门。
3
Pull out the detergent drawer (top left) and add detergent; add fabric softener if desired. Do not exceed the MAX line.拉出洗涤剂盒（左上方），加入洗衣液；如需要可加入柔顺剂。请勿超过MAX刻度线。
4
Turn the program dial: Cotton (everyday items), Synthetics (lightweight garments), 15' Quick Wash, or Rinse + Spin.旋转程序旋钮：棉质（日常衣物）、化纤（轻薄衣物）、15分钟快洗或漂洗+脱水。
Drying (Optional) 烘干（可选）
1
After washing, turn the dial to Cotton Dry or Synthetics Dry, then press Start/Pause.洗涤后，将旋钮转至棉质烘干或化纤烘干，然后按启动/暂停。
2
The machine beeps when finished — open the door and remove laundry.完成后机器会发出提示音——打开机门取出衣物。
⚠️ Always empty pockets before washing. Clothes may be hot right after drying. If you hear unusual noise or smell burning, stop and contact your host.洗涤前请务必清空口袋。烘干后衣物可能较烫。若听到异响或闻到烧焦味，请停止使用并联系房东。

02 · Kitchen
Bosch Built-In Oven
博世嵌入式烤箱
Model: HBA534BS0A / Type: HR5B20F0
How to Use 使用方法
1
Turn the left dial to choose a cooking mode: 3D Hot Air, Top & Bottom Heat, Grill, or Pizza Mode.转动左侧旋钮选择烹饪模式：3D热风、上下加热、烧烤或披萨模式。
2
Turn the right dial to set temperature — 160–200°C for baking/roasting, 200–250°C for pizza/grilling.转动右侧旋钮设定温度——烘烤/烤肉160–200°C，披萨/烧烤200–250°C。
3
Press Timer, use + / − to set cooking time, then press Start/Stop to confirm.按定时键，用+/−设定烹饪时间，按启动/停止确认。
4
When done, turn the left dial back to Off (●) and remove food using oven mitts.完成后将左侧旋钮转回关闭（●）位置，使用隔热手套取出食物。
⚠️ Always use oven mitts. Do not place plastic containers inside. If you smell burning, turn off the oven and inform your host.务必使用隔热手套。请勿将塑料容器放入烤箱内。若闻到烧焦味，请关闭烤箱并告知房东。

03 · Kitchen
Bosch Hob & Extractor Hood
博世灶台与抽油烟机
Model: PKK651FP1E
Hob 灶台
1
Touch ⏻ (Main Switch) to power on — all displays light up.触摸⏻（总开关）开机——所有显示灯亮起。
2
Touch the ◎ symbol for your chosen burner, then tap a number 1 (low) to 9 (high) to set heat.触摸对应炉头的◎符号，然后点选1（弱）至9（强）设定火力。
3
Tap P for PowerBoost to bring water to a boil extra fast.点击P启动加速沸腾功能，可更快烧开水。
4
To stop a zone, select it and tap 0. To switch off the whole hob, touch ⏻. Wait for the "H" symbol to disappear before cleaning.关闭单个炉头：选中后点击0。关闭整个灶台：触摸⏻。清洁前请等待"H"符号消失。
Extractor Hood 抽油烟机
1
Press Power to start the fan. Choose Speed 1 (low), 2 (medium), or 3 (high).按电源键启动风扇。选择1档（弱）、2档（中）或3档（强）。
2
Press Light to turn the hood light on or off.按照明键开关抽油烟机灯光。
⚠️ Never leave cooking unattended. Use flat-bottom cookware only. If the glass is cracked or you smell gas, switch off the main fuse and contact your host immediately.烹饪时切勿离开。请仅使用平底炊具。若玻璃面板破裂或闻到煤气味，请立即关闭总保险丝并联系房东。

04 · Comfort
Samsung Air Conditioner
三星空调
Digital Inverter Split Unit (1.5HP / 2.5HP)
Operation 操作方法
1
Point the remote at the indoor unit and press Power.将遥控器对准室内机，按电源键。
2
Press Mode to choose: Cool / Dry / Fan / Auto. Recommended: Cool for daily use.按模式键选择：制冷/除湿/送风/自动。日常建议使用制冷模式。
3
Press Temp + / − to set temperature. Recommended range: 24–26°C.按温度+/−设定温度。建议范围：24–26°C。
4
Press Fan </> for fan speed, or Blade Direction for airflow/swing.按风速键调整风速，或按导风板键调整出风方向/摇摆。
5
Optional: Turbo (fast cooling), Quiet (low noise), Good Sleep (night comfort), Auto Clean (dries interior to prevent odor).可选功能：速冷、静音、安睡模式、自动清洁（烘干内部防止异味）。
⚠️ Don't block the air vents or touch the remote with wet hands. After a power cut, wait 2–3 minutes before restarting.请勿阻挡出风口，也请勿用湿手操作遥控器。停电后请等待2–3分钟再重新开机。

06 · Kitchen
Electrolux Microwave
伊莱克斯微波炉
Model: EMG23K22B
How to Use 使用方法
1
Place food in a microwave-safe container in the centre of the turntable. Avoid metal or foil.将食物放入微波炉专用容器，置于转盘中央。请勿使用金属或铝箔。
2
Turn the top knob to set power: Quick Cook (100%/800W), Reheat (~80%), Simmer (~50–60%), Defrost (~30%), or Melt (~15%).转动顶部旋钮设定功率：快速加热（100%/800W）、复热（约80%）、慢炖（约50–60%）、解冻（约30%）或融化（约15%）。
3
Turn the bottom knob to set time (1–35 min) — it starts automatically.转动底部旋钮设定时间（1–35分钟）——设定后自动开始。
4
The microwave dings when finished. Use a cloth or oven mitt to remove hot containers.完成后微波炉会发出提示音。请用布或隔热手套取出热容器。
⚠️ Never run the microwave when empty, and never place metal inside. If you see smoke or sparks, unplug immediately.请勿空转微波炉，切勿放入金属物品。若发现冒烟或火花，请立即拔掉插头。

07 · Kitchen
Philips Rice Cooker
飞利浦电饭锅
Model: E7RC1650K
Cooking Rice 煮饭方法
1
Wash rice and add to the inner pot with water to the correct level line. Wipe the pot's exterior dry, then place it in the cooker.淘洗大米，放入内锅并按刻度线加水。擦干内锅外部，放入电饭锅中。
2
Close the lid and plug in. Press M to choose a program: Fast Cook / White Rice / Congee / Soup / Cake / Brown Rice / Steam / Keep Warm.盖好锅盖并插上电源。按M键选择程序：快煮/白米饭/粥/汤/蛋糕/糙米/蒸煮/保温。
3
Use L to adjust time where applicable, then press O to start.如适用，用L键调整时间，然后按O键开始。
4
The cooker beeps six times when done and switches to Keep Warm automatically.完成后电饭锅会发出六声提示音，并自动切换至保温模式。
💡 Use only the rice paddle provided — no metal utensils.请仅使用随附的饭勺，勿使用金属器具。

14 · Laundry
Philips Steam Iron
飞利浦蒸汽熨斗
Model: DST3040
How to Use 使用方法
1
Fill the water tank to the MAX level for steam ironing.加水至MAX刻度以进行蒸汽熨烫。
2
Set the temperature dial according to the fabric type (check the garment label).根据布料类型设定温度旋钮（请查看衣物标签）。
3
Wait for the ready indicator light to turn off before ironing.待指示灯熄灭后再开始熨烫。
4
Press the steam button for extra steam, or use the spray function for stubborn creases.按蒸汽键释放额外蒸汽，或使用喷雾功能处理顽固褶皱。
5
After use, turn the temperature dial to Min/Off and empty any remaining water.使用后将温度旋钮转至最低/关闭位置，并倒空剩余水分。
⚠️ Always stand the iron upright on its heel when not in use. Never leave it unattended while hot, and keep out of reach of children.不使用时请务必将熨斗竖立放置。发热时切勿离开，并请放在儿童无法触及之处。
